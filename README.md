The open passport spread of the Republic of Velmora, an island nation that does not exist. The data page belongs to Elin Marek Solen, lighthouse keeper of Sarelle, with a portrait, a machine-readable zone, and issue details from 2 June 2026. The visas page carries her travel history through Karstovia and Tessalin in the summer and autumn of 2026, and the harbour-fee stamp that pays for the Outer Isles.

The image is drawn by `draw_entry.py` (Python + PIL, outside the entry program) and shipped as `entry.png`. `main.ts` copies it to the output as `/output/entry.png` after checking it is a whole PNG of at most 1 MiB and 1024 by 1024 pixels.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
